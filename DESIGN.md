# Briefsona design specification
Working name, subject to change before retail. Tagline: The ultimate news experience. Developer: MacGuyverism. Approved nameless launcher icon packaged from Icon.png. Proposed package com.macguyverism.briefsona.
## v0.0.1
Kotlin, Compose, Media3 ExoPlayer/MediaSessionService, WorkManager, Room, DataStore, Android 8+, portrait and landscape. Existing Python production engine, GitHub Actions, Supabase. MP3 64 kbps mono, ID3 CHAP/CTOC and separate JSON chapter metadata. Chapter timings include cues, overlaps and tempo adjustments.
Radio dashboard: automotive styling, station dropdown, rolling seven weekday/date buttons, edition, Bluetooth indicator, segment/reporter, waveform/progress, elapsed/total, previous/next chapter, 15s rewind, 30s forward, play, horizontal presets (two full and partial third), expandable All Segments, speed, offline status. No gear or refresh on dashboard. Bottom navigation Stations / Radio (default) / Settings.
Backgrounds: Charcoal #151517/card #262628; Midnight Blue #141c26/card #242f3c; Dark Espresso #211a16/card #332820. Independent illumination: Amber #ffb340 default, Ice Blue #55caff, Sport Red #ff4949, Emerald #51e69a, Purple #b58aff, White #eeeeee.
Offline playback, background audio, Bluetooth and lock screen controls, resume default on. Automatic download default on, WiFi-only default off. Local retention 1–7 days default 7; cloud fixed seven days (rolling calendar days proposed). Local deletion does not delete cloud. Device and app storage breakdown. Download missing episodes newest first, verify before playback, do not interrupt active playback. Date indicators green downloaded, blue available remotely, both possible; dim unavailable. Multiple editions on date open selection.
Settings: Appearance, Playback (0.75/1/1.25/1.5/1.75/2x), Downloads, Storage, Connection, About. System timezone initially. Stations list/default station/status/retrieval initially; editing later.
## Production and reliability
Production begins 45 minutes before release; stage until release. Refresh on launch, stale foreground, release, network restoration; retry retrieval every five minutes for one hour. Active production delayed rather than duplicate retry. Manual retry in Stations only until next production begins. Separate production retry and download retry. One-line status Scheduled/In progress/Awaiting release/Ready/Delayed/Failed/Expired; backend heartbeat. Keep prior episode on failure. Prevent duplicate scheduled production before paid AI calls; intentional manual regeneration allowed. Deduplicate URLs, identical articles and overlapping stories, preserving independent corroboration. Use accessible full articles rather than headlines alone; respect access restrictions, no paywall bypass. Source failure must not unnecessarily abort entire broadcast. Target duration rather than padding: remove mandatory minimum segment word counts, prioritize verified facts, figures, context and meaningful developments; avoid repetitive generic commentary. Shorter substantive episodes preferable to filler.
## Editorial and sources
Preferred publishers prioritized, not exclusive. Consult supplemental publishers and discovery services routinely. Seek credible competing political perspectives, distinguish facts, disputed claims and opinion, avoid false equivalence. Transparent editorial quality standard; marketing focuses on creating your own news and reporters. Source candidates: AP, Reuters, Fox News, CNN, NPR, Newsmax, The Hill, Politico, Washington Examiner, BBC, Al Jazeera, DW, CNBC, Fox Business, Yahoo Finance, NASA, ScienceDaily, Scientific American, Space.com, Universe Today, Ars Technica, TechCrunch, Wired, Hak5, The Hacker News, KrebsOnSecurity, BleepingComputer. Verify feeds and article retrieval before enabling. Personal Rhode Island sources include Valley Breeze, The Observer, WPRI, NBC10, Rhode Island Current, ABC6; local publishers are personal add-ins, not hardcoded universal defaults. Personal finance news uses manually supplied ticker lists, no brokerage connection.
## Later versions
Local ZIP-based RSS discovery explicitly deferred beyond v0.0.1; discover regional publishers, offer selectable feeds. Custom RSS URL add/remove/enable/disable and test connection also deferred. Exact version TBD.
Station creation/editing, reusable reporter library, voice preview, personality, pace, vocabulary, humor, structured and raw Markdown editor with formatted preview. Station copy inside individual station page; manual/automatic schedule, days, release time, produce now with cost confirmation. Duration 5–60 minutes; one customizable segment per three minutes, opening/closing mandatory first/last but editable; relative duration weights, fixed/random order, editable sound bites and volume/fade. Free 5–10 minutes, max three custom segments and two saved stations; Pro up to 60 minutes and 20 custom segments.
Commercial: provider-neutral architecture, optional BYOK secure encrypted backend credentials, private per-user accounts/storage/settings/schedules/usage, usage caps, Google Play billing, account deletion/export, licensed cues, AI disclosure. No embedded secrets. Pricing deferred.
v0.5.0 target: proprietary .bsa encrypted MP3 container, authenticated seekable AES-256-GCM blocks, Android Keystore protected keys, app-private downloads, offline playback without plaintext files. Discourage casual ad bypass, not impenetrable DRM. Embedded audio ads for free broadcasts, app restricts skipping during ads, optional unobtrusive visual ads outside playback; paid ad-free. No full-screen driving ads. Suggested 15–30 second ad per free broadcast; final ad policy TBD.

## Testing and implementation status
Development and the Android debug build are authorized on feature/article-chapters-reliability. The backend now extracts publisher reporting, groups similar stories, reserves each scheduled production date, stages until release and validates both chapter formats. Android reads the seven-day archive with a legacy latest.json fallback.

The approved launcher artwork features a microphone, headphones, amber globe and newspaper. The Android adaptive launcher resource uses the original Icon.png from main commit 0694700. Radio, Stations and Settings follow the supplied references. Illustrative dates, stations, segments and status values in the references are populated from the available broadcasts.

A feature-branch test broadcast requires editorial review before promotion. Verify downloads, offline and background playback, Bluetooth, lock screen controls, resume, chapter navigation, retention, all 18 themes and landscape on Kayla and Jade.
